FAQ

Common questions before you start.

Do I need resource consent?

It depends on the property, proposal and applicable planning rules. Pathway is designed to organise the information needed to understand the likely pathway and flag where professional review is required.

Is Pathway a planner?

No. Pathway is a platform for property intelligence, application preparation, professional matching and project management. Professional judgement remains with the relevant qualified professional.

Can I use my own planner or architect?

That is part of the intended product model. Customers should be able to invite an existing professional or choose from the Pathway marketplace.

How much will my project cost?

Professional, specialist and council costs vary. Pathway aims to show expected ranges and obtain comparable quotes before work is approved.

What if council asks for more information?

The planned RFI workflow breaks the request into tasks, assigns the right professional and keeps the response status visible to the customer.

Does Pathway handle building consent too?

The long-term product is designed to continue from planning/resource consent into building consent, inspections, conditions and CCC rather than stopping at one approval.

What councils are supported?

The current prototype is Auckland-first. Wider New Zealand support should be added council-by-council with validated data and rule coverage.
