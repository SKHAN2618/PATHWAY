This prototype page is not final legal advice. Before launch, Pathway needs a New Zealand privacy policy covering account data, property/project information, uploaded documents, professional marketplace data, communications, payment providers, analytics and retention.

Principles for the production policy

Collect only what is needed, explain why it is collected, restrict access by project role, protect sensitive project documents, give users appropriate access/correction rights and define retention/deletion practices.
