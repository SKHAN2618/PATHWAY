Trust & sources

See what information was checked — and what still needs verification.

Pathway should never hide uncertainty behind one generic “verified” badge.

1 · Council spatial data

Property address, zoning, precincts, overlays, controls and designations can be sourced from council GIS services where available.

2 · Statutory plan checks

Mapped information is not the same as reading the operative planning provisions, objectives, policies, rules and standards that apply.

3 · Professional review

Important planning conclusions, design decisions and technical certifications should remain reviewable by the appropriate professional.

For the Auckland pilot

The prototype is designed around Auckland Council public GIS / GeoMaps-style spatial information, with the long-term architecture separating those spatial findings from the current Auckland Unitary Plan text and any relevant plan changes.
