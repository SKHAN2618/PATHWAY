HOW IT WORKS

From “Can I build this?” to a professionally managed approval pathway.

The app is designed so the customer never has to understand the entire planning industry before they can start.

1

Describe the idea

Address, normal-language description, photos and whatever plans they already have.

2

Feasibility + research

The planning intelligence layer surfaces zoning, overlays, applicable provisions, likely activity status, consent triggers and missing information.

3

Draft + professional review

Pathway prepares the working AEE and application material, then a verified planner or designer reviews and completes the professional work.

4

Choose + pay

Compare verified professionals, approve transparent service milestones and keep the commercial side inside the project.

5

Lodge + manage council

Files, completeness checks, lodgement readiness, council correspondence and RFIs stay attached to one project record.

6

Continue beyond resource consent

The long-term Pathway vision carries the same project into building consent, inspections, conditions and CCC rather than stopping at the planning decision.
