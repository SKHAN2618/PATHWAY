HOW IT WORKS

From “Can I build this?” to a managed approval pathway.

The app is designed so the customer never has to understand the entire planning industry before they can start.

1

Describe the idea

Address, normal-language description, photos and whatever plans they already have.

2

Generate a pathway

AI organises likely constraints, next steps, missing information and the appropriate professional category.

3

Professional review

Verified planners or designers can claim or quote the work through the platform.

4

In-app payment

Customer approves transparent service milestones. Platform handles payment and professional payout.

5

Application tracking

Files, status changes, messages, council requests and outstanding tasks live in one project.

6

Outcome

Application progresses to decision, then can continue into building consent and completion workflows.
