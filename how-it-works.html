How it works

From “Can I build this?” to a managed approval pathway.

Pathway is designed so you can start without understanding the planning system first.

1

Tell us the property

Enter an address and explain your idea in everyday language.

2

Get the first answer

For the Auckland pilot, Pathway can use council address and spatial-map data to show the zone, selected mapped constraints and a preliminary answer before signup.

3

Go deeper when needed

Save the project and move into rule-by-rule assessment, source tracking, scenario comparison and the information a professional needs to review.

4

Choose and work with professionals

Match by specialty, location, budget, response time and online / in-person preference, then keep the scope, documents and messages attached to the project.

5

Prepare and track approvals

Organise plans, reports and the AEE Draft for Professional Review, then follow lodgement, council requests, decisions and conditions.

6

Keep going

Continue the same project into building consent, inspections, producer statements and CCC where relevant.

Inside a Pathway project

Property + planning

Property record, source verification, planning findings, risk flags, scenarios and readiness.

Delivery

Professional matching, quotes, milestones, files, document versions, tasks and project messages.

Approvals

Resource consent, council RFIs, conditions, building consent, inspections and CCC tracking.

You do not need to know the process before you start.

Start with the property and the idea. Pathway shows what should happen next.
