How it works

From “Can I build this?” to a managed approval pathway.

Pathway is designed so you can start without understanding the planning system first.

1

Tell us the property

Enter an address and explain your idea in everyday language.

2

Understand the pathway

Pathway organises available property information, likely constraints, missing information and next steps.

3

Choose professional help

Compare suitable planners, designers, surveyors, engineers and other specialists.

4

Prepare the application

Keep the project brief, AEE draft for review, plans, reports and source trail together.

5

Track council

Follow lodgement, requests for information, assigned tasks, decisions and conditions.

6

Keep going

Continue the same project into building consent, inspections, producer statements and CCC where relevant.

You do not need to know the process before you start.

Start with the property and the idea. Pathway shows what should happen next.
