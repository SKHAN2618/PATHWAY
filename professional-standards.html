Professional standards

Pathway prepares and organises. Professionals handle professional judgement.

The platform should make it clear which outputs are informational or draft material and which work has been reviewed, certified or delivered by an appropriate professional.

Verified credentials

Profiles should accurately describe qualifications, memberships, licences or registrations relevant to that profession.

Clear responsibility

Draft research, AEE material and automated checks remain reviewable until accepted by the responsible professional.

Audit trail

Important project conclusions should retain the source, version, date and professional review/override history.
