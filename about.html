About Pathway

Property approvals are fragmented. Pathway brings the process together.

Today, property owners jump between council information, planners, designers, engineers, reports, email threads, invoices and approval portals. Pathway is being designed as one place to understand and manage that journey.

Our mission

Make getting something built in New Zealand significantly easier to understand and manage.

Our principle

Technology should handle repetitive research, organisation and administration while professional judgement stays with professionals.

Our direction

Start with property feasibility and resource consent, then carry the same project through building consent, conditions, inspections and CCC.

Current focus

Auckland property pilot

Use Auckland Council public address and spatial services as the first live property-data foundation, with clear source status and verification boundaries.

Rule evidence engine

Connect mapped findings to the applicable plan provisions, standards, activity status, plan changes and professional review rather than relying on a generic answer.

Two role-specific products

Customers manage their approval journey. Professionals receive structured work and deliver it through a separate professional workspace.

What Pathway is

A marketplace and operating system for property approvals.

Customers get a clearer journey from feasibility to approval. Professionals get better-structured briefs and delivery tools. The project keeps one source of truth instead of being split across council maps, inboxes, PDFs, quotes and portals.

What Pathway is not

Not a replacement for professional judgement.

Planning, design, engineering and certification responsibilities remain with the appropriate qualified professionals.
